Safe Passage

A small moment in Meldan. A stranger, a flying toy, and a promise that reveals only what it needs to.

The scene

Gladias walks beneath the trees of Meldan. A toy drone sweeps overhead. His watch confirms that the drone is a toy with safe navigation, while keeping unrelated information private. A child catches it, and the quiet walk continues.

This short interprets the opening of chapter 1. Its visual design, timing and sound are original adaptation choices.

Make it your own

The source package includes deterministic scene code, sound synthesis, the renderer, a storyboard, production prompts and rebuild instructions. No paid API or borrowed music is needed to reproduce it.

Made with AI assistance through OpenAI Codex, Node.js, sharp and FFmpeg. No voice performance or endorsement by the novel’s author is implied.

Audio and scene guide

  • 0–8s: trees, stone path, quiet ambience; Gladias walks.
  • 8–13s: the three-rotor toy passes overhead.
  • 13–19s: a close view of the watch’s two safety checks.
  • 19–27s: the child reaches, jumps and catches the toy.
  • 27–36s: Gladias continues; the title and credits appear.

There is no spoken dialogue. The story is conveyed visually; this guide describes the main action.
